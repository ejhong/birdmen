Opus ranked the pair highly; GPT-5.5 preferred many alternatives. Both results remain in the record. Some responses misread the moai’s back as a frontal figure, while the prompts mixed visible resemblance with shared-origin judgments.

Indus & rongorongo

Rendering style changes the rankings. In independent-rendition validation, the combined metric retrieved the correct sign first in 75.4% of queries, below HOG alone at 80.5%; the embedding component reached 11.6%.

The civilisers

Claimed partners ranked first in 248 of 397 completed lineups. The summaries were recognisable: Opus identified 96 of 103 regions in a recognition check. Repeated lineups and mismatched roles weaken the original chance comparison.

The V2 audit checks 29 source passages; it did not rerun the rankings. A better pilot would separate generic roles from specific event sequences and show the strongest alternative matches.

The raven & the dove

One model’s coding of compiled summaries shows regional patterns. Post-contact recording does not prove borrowing; regional structure does not exclude older inheritance.

Trace witnesses, independently code specific sequences, and separate related versions. Which birds leave, in what order and with what result is more precise than “animals occur”.
