The proposed connection

What makes it a question?

The same compact outline is pointed out across ancient Anatolia, Assyria and Mesoamerica. The time gaps and distant contexts motivate the proposed anomaly.

Seven explicitly develops the Göbekli Tepe / West Asian handled-form proposal. The project archive preserves the extension to La Venta; the earliest proposer of that three-way version is untraced.
Secret of the Gods: stone handbags

Look closely

What appears to correspond

  • An arch or handle rises over a roughly enclosed lower form in the selected images.
Keep in the frame

What differs or remains uncertain

  • Pillar 43’s forms are arranged overhead and are not visibly carried.
  • The Nimrud bucket belongs to a cone-and-bucket ritual ensemble.
  • La Venta’s small carried object has its own local interpretation; shared shape does not establish shared function.
Transmission check

No route established in this review

No transmission sequence for this specific comparison has been established in the sources reviewed. That is not proof that contact was impossible.

What would move this forward

  • Collect handled forms and plausible non-container alternatives without selecting only good matches.
  • Trace each interpretation to an object-specific source.
