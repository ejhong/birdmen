What makes it a question?

Lists of teachers, civilisers and sea journeys suggest a shared story. Source-level comparison asks which elements actually travel together.

The project’s source audit tests the familiar proposed “civilisers” bundle. Hancock’s broader proposal is linked as context rather than as authorship of each formulation.
Teaching figures: primary-source audit

Look closely

What appears to correspond

  • Teaching or institutions are associated with each figure.
  • Water or sea departures occur in different parts of the selected accounts.
Keep in the frame

What differs or remains uncertain

  • Arrival from the sea, nightly return to the sea and final departure across it are different events.
  • Recording dates are not the dates at which the traditions began.
  • The compared accounts do not establish one matching promise of personal return.
Transmission check

No route established in this review

No transmission sequence for this specific comparison has been established in the sources reviewed. That is not proof that contact was impossible.

What would move this forward

  • Expand local versions and indigenous-language witnesses.
  • Blindly code event sequences with independently checked translations.
