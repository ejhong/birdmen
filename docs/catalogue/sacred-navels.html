The proposed connection

What makes it a question?

A rounded sacred stone and the metaphor of a world’s navel could suggest a shared concept, if both associations can be established historically.

This pairing is preserved in the project archive. Newman’s article provides a broader proposed “world navels” comparison, not the object-level proof.

Look closely

What appears to correspond

  • Modern presentations associate both places with a navel or sacred centre.
Keep in the frame

What differs or remains uncertain

  • The Delphi object is shaped and decorated; the Rapanui stone’s modification and placement history need checking.
  • An island name does not date the modern identification of a particular stone.
Transmission check

Transmission not yet assessed

The comparison is not yet identified or dated well enough to exclude ordinary transmission, later borrowing or an image-selection effect.

What would move this forward

  • Find early Rapanui-language and collection witnesses.
