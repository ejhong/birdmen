Sources & scope

Source-led catalogue extension, not an independent specialist review.

  1. supplied material · Supplied project archive

    Input coverage and identification audit

    Every supplied file is accounted for. Coverage is separate from verification; unresolved montages remain leads.

  2. project · Deep Memory

    Supplied-image identity register

    Preserves captions, uncertainties and supplied images. A social caption is not an independent archaeological identification.

  3. museum · The Metropolitan Museum of Art

    Eagle-headed relief · 32.143.7

    Exact object pictured here: Nimrud, reign of Ashurnasirpal II, ca. 883–859 BCE. Bucket and cone in a protective ritual context.

  4. museum · British Museum

    Hoa Hakananai’a: collection record

    Identifies the statue and back carvings; construction and later carving episodes must be separated.

  5. photograph · Dennis G. Jarvis

    Gate of the Sun, central figure and attendant rows (detail)

    An openly licensed detail of the carved lintel. The uploader's identification of the central figure as Viracocha is a traditional reading, not an inscription.

  6. museum · British Museum

    Moai: objects, histories and Rapanui perspectives

    Museum account gives an early traditional statue date and a later birdman tradition. Its settlement chronology differs from recent research.

  7. research · University of Southampton

    Hoa Hakananai’a: 3D and reflectance imaging

    Project page for surface-recording work. The older interactive viewer returned 403 during this review; its underlying data were not examined.

  8. museum · British Museum / Google Arts & Culture

    Birdman boulder · Oc1920,0506.1

    Orongo provenance, birdman with rounded object, and an approximate 1750–1875 catalogue date.

  9. primary-text · Katherine Routledge

    The Mystery of Easter Island (1919)

    Figures 110 and 112 preserve photographs of the Orongo rock and birdman boulder. Publication dates are not carving dates.

  10. museum · Museo Antropológico Padre Sebastián Englert

    Painted slabs from Orongo · MAPSE 17-00-1312

    Two facing tangata manu on a house slab; the 1974 move to the museum does not date the painting.
