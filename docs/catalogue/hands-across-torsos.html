The proposed connection

What makes it a question?

A similar resting arrangement is proposed as a persistent convention across disconnected sculptural traditions.

Newman explicitly compares Urfa Man with distant torso gestures including moai. The Ponce example is the project’s additional proposed comparison, not a claim that Newman identified this exact statue.
Megalithic origins: Göbekli Tepe and Peru

Look closely

What appears to correspond

  • Arms descend beside a frontal body; the hands converge or act near the torso.
Keep in the frame

What differs or remains uncertain

  • The Ponce figure holds objects; the other poses need separate descriptions.
  • The hands are not necessarily directed at the same anatomical point.
  • Similar human bodies constrain the number of comfortable or legible sculptural poses.
Transmission check

No route established in this review

No transmission sequence for this specific comparison has been established in the sources reviewed. That is not proof that contact was impossible.

What would move this forward

  • Trace object dates and acquisition identities.
  • Code exact hand positions, held objects and local sculptural predecessors.

    Museum account gives an early traditional statue date and a later birdman tradition. Its settlement chronology differs from recent research.

  5. research · Moreno-Mayar et al., 2024

    Ancient Rapanui genomes and contact with the Americas

    Ancient-genome evidence supports pre-European admixture dated about 1250–1430 CE. It does not establish a route for a particular motif.
