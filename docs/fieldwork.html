What counts as a shared feature?

Beaked figure — A beaked head on the selected figure; a museum deity name alone is insufficient.

Arm or wing extends forward — A limb projects in front of the selected beaked figure. An arm and a wing remain different anatomical readings.

Separate rounded form — A disc, ring or compact rounded object, excluding an eye, body outline or suspension hole.

Rounded form at the limb — The same beaked figure’s arm or wing reaches to the rounded form. Nearness does not prove a grip or shared meaning.

Facing beaked figures — Two beaked figures face one another within the same scene. Separate objects never create this feature.

These definitions were developed while inspecting the material. This is an exploratory reading exercise, not a preregistered similarity test. Agreement between AI readers would not substitute for independent archaeological review.

04 / Competing explanations

What would change the answer?

Local development and recurrence

What we would expect
Local variants connect the figures to particular practices; broad ingredients recur with different arrangements and meanings.
Evidence in hand
Local bird repertoires are documented on both sides. The egg has a Rapanui historical context; the Neolithic disc remains ambiguous.
Still missing
A fuller regional sequence and a defined comparison collection are still needed. Local context alone does not rule out older inheritance.
The next useful check
Acquire further dated Orongo scenes and nearby Anatolian bird compositions, preserving less similar examples.

Transmission through intermediates

What we would expect
Several specific details recur together in a dated sequence, with independent evidence supporting the proposed connections.
Evidence in hand
Pacific–American contact has independent support. The selected Egyptian and Assyrian parallels are comparisons, not a demonstrated route from Anatolia.
Still missing
No connected sequence for this precise motif has been established by this investigation.
The next useful check
Search dated intermediates for the whole arrangement, including its unusual differences, and log unsuccessful searches.

A much older shared inheritance
