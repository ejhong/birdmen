# Investigations

Deep Memory keeps every investigation available on equal terms: a question, an identifiable
body of evidence, findings, limitations, and a route back to the underlying work. A version
label describes a method, not a ranking of a study's importance.

New investigations belong in a named directory here. Keep the method, source register,
evidence and interpretation together. Separate observations from inferences and proposed
work from completed work. Do not overwrite a previous version's data or preregistration.

The original pipelines and datasets retain their paths (`pipeline/`, `data/`) so that their
relative imports, recorded commands and published links continue to work. The repository
README maps those paths to the corresponding investigation.

## Standards for a source-led investigation

1. Link each assertion to a source and a passage locator. Distinguish the source's words
   from a translation, paraphrase or interpretation.
2. Record composition, surviving witness and edition dates separately when known. An
   account's recording date is not the date when a tradition began.
3. Preserve variants. Do not manufacture a single biography from contradictory accounts.
4. Treat "not mentioned here" differently from contradiction or historical absence.
5. Record dependence: copied passages, shared source articles and translations of one
   witness are not independent confirmations.
6. Use AI for retrieval and extraction; keep its judgments inspectable and correctable.
7. Label exploratory comparisons honestly. Do not turn a selected collection of appealing
   examples into a probability claim about all traditions.
